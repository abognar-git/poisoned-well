ACT I

The machine that never sleeps

On 20 March 2024, a website called pravda-hu.com appeared. No journalists. No office. No readers to speak of. It has not stopped publishing since.

I·1 — THE SWITCH FLIPS

The Hungarian mirror went live in a 31-domain wave that hit 19 EU countries in a single week — documented by France's VIGINUM. From a single article on launch day, output passed 250 a day within a month — and has cleared 100 on 531 of its 869 days since.

I·2 — INDUSTRIAL SCALE

No human newsroom works like this. Output is relentless, around the clock — peaking at 712 articles in one day. The bet isn't that Hungarians will read it. It's that search engines and AI models will.

I·3 — THE ELECTION

As Hungary's April 2026 vote approached, the wider operation escalated: GRU-linked operatives reported in Budapest, fake outlets, deepfakes. The mirror kept feeding the machines through it all.

I·4 — THE VOTE THAT CHANGED NOTHING

Orbán lost. The government changed. The campaigns were debunked. And the mirror? It published — articles today. The election was never the endgame.

I·5 — WHERE IT COMES FROM

The mirror writes nothing. It launders — scraping fringe Hungarian-language Telegram channels and Russian state outlets, re-publishing them as clean, crawlable "news" with an average of 2.8 language versions per article.
